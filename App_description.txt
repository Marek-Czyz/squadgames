Make an webb-app (preferably react-app.

General

The app shall utilize colors and schemes provided in the folder "Assets"
The app shall have a logo provided in "Assets"
The app will be hosted in firebase and requires simple login (e-mail and password)
The data of this app will be kept in firebase database (like the score of the game)
The app will be used by 5-10 teams. The data structure will be created on the first login of a new "user".
The user will be asked to enter the team name, then the database structure for this team will be estabilished.


The app functionality:

After login the app will show the name of the team logged inn, and their current credit in valuta "fc" on the top

The app will facilitate a live game, that consist of 5 different "posts". The posts will be presented in the main view as 5 selectable "cards". When selected card, the view of this particular post is shown and a button "back" to get back to the main menu.

The posts:

1. " What is this? What is what?"

This task is about quessing the name of an object. The screen will show a simple text entry, and a button "quess". The correct answer (or list of) will be provided in a secret file in ../Secrets/post1.ini
When the name is quessed correctly the credit ncreases by 50fc and the post gets closed for that team - field grayed out (not possible to guess again), if the answer is not correct the screen says "incorrect, next guess in: <counts down 59s>".

Below the text box place two buttons "buy time" and "buy hint". The first button deducts 5fc form the score, the second button deducts 10fc from the score and reveals a hint in order, provided in the file ../Secrets/hint1.ini  



2. "The art of engineering"

This post will be handled manually by a host that can "grant" money to a team after fullfilling it. The page shall show a number field where the host may write the amount of fc's (may also be negative) then a field for password, and a button "grant". When the admin enters the correct password, provided in ../Secrets/post2.ini, the amount of credits entered is added to the pot of this team. This can be done several times, but the password must be re-entered every time.

3. "Knowledge is king"

This page shows a list of 12 cards, 6 questions, 6 riddles. Make the riddles and questions utilize different colors, use gradient according to their "price":

   - Question 1 (3fc)
   - Question 2 (5fc)
   - Question 3 (10fc)
   - Question 4 (10fc)
   - Question 5 (10fc)
   - Question 6 (10fc) 

   - Riddle 1 (10fc)
   - Riddle 2 (15fc)
   - Riddle 3 (20fc)
   - Riddle 4 (30fc)
   - Riddle 5 (50fc)
   - Riddle 6 (60fc)

When selecting a card, the pop up asks "Do you really want to buy this?" and Yes No - buttons. When yes selected the amount of fc is deducted from the account, and the Question/Riddle is revealed. The questions are multi choice selectable with options A,B,C,D, the riddles has a text fiekld to provide a correct answer.

The list of questions and riddles is provided in ../Secrets/questions.ini
The correct answers in ../Secrets/answers.ini

If the answer is provided correctly, the amount is doubled back (i.e. 10fc deducted, +20fc granted) and the question is disabled (grayed out). If the answer is incorrect, the question goes back to the "hidden" mode, and the team may "purchase" it again same way as before.
